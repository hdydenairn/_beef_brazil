Study of impact of foreign demand shocks on deforestation.

Specifcally, the sudden increase of demand for beef in China, caused by exogenous negative shock to pork supply, resulting in increased beef consumption, and in turn exports of beef from Brazil to China. There is no evidence of a 'direct' (Brazilian firms exporting to China) causal effect on deforestation, however when considering substition and industry reorganisation effects, the impact is unclear.
